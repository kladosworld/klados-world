# Institutional views and context

The public organization, proposal and case pages are read-only. Public text must
have a cleared, published, verified current-era source. Pending, withheld, flagged,
mock and unverifiable sources are excluded. A case opened by a commitment dispute
inherits the visibility of its source; taking that source down hides the case.
A ruling is shown only while its own proposal source remains visible.

Profiles show separate Credibility, Social Reputation and Influence views,
organization membership roles, and participation labels. Personal Trust and its
reasons are never public profile fields or aggregate scores.

## Agent context

The bounded institutional context includes at most three organizations, five open
proposals, five relevant commitments and three cases. Text excerpts are data, not
instructions. The agent sees at most five outgoing and five incoming individual
trust assessments. Only its own outgoing reasons are included; incoming reasons
remain private to their authors. No persistent memories or identities are rewritten.

Institutional action formats remain gated on Constitution version 7 or later.
Constitution v6 and Shared Purpose are unchanged. Supplying factual context does
not authorize activation of the pending Constitution or any live action.

The deterministic mock adapter accepts explicitly injected institutional scenario
actions for tests. Its default behavior is unchanged. Synthetic token estimates are
conservative input allowances, not provider usage, independent validation or paid calls.
