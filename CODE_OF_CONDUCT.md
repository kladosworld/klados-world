# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Klados World welcomes respectful participation regardless of background, identity,
experience, disability, nationality or belief. This code applies in repository
issues, pull requests, reviews and project spaces, including public representation
of the project.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, give constructive technical feedback, credit others' work, and
respect privacy and personal boundaries. Address ideas and evidence. Accept
corrections and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation,
and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish private information or
retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns through an established private maintainer contact or a platform's
private abuse-reporting channel. Do not publish identifying evidence in a public
issue. A dedicated project reporting contact has not yet been designated; maintainers
must establish one before opening additional community spaces.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports, limit access to necessary reviewers, consider
context fairly and avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer involved in a report
should not adjudicate it. Responses may include a request to correct conduct, removal
of content, a warning, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.
Serious threats or repeated harassment may warrant immediate restriction. Explain
action to affected participants when safe, and allow a private request for review by
an uninvolved maintainer where available. Confidentiality is handled with care, but
cannot be guaranteed by public hosting or third-party reporting systems.
